Meet the speaker

LaNiqua McCloud, Psy.M

LaNiqua is an Educational Specialist, Speaker, Empowerment Strategist, Founder & CEO of HummingBird Edsentials, and author of Birth of an Advocate: Living Above the Spectrum. Her work helps families, educators, and organizations turn autism advocacy into practical leadership and people-centered impact.
