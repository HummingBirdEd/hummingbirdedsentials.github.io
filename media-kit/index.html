Short bio

Educational Specialist | Speaker | Master Trainer | Autism Family Advocacy Strategist

LaNiqua McCloud, Psy.M is an Educational Specialist, Speaker, Autism Family Consultant, Founder & CEO of HummingBird Edsentials, and author of Birth of an Advocate: Living Above the Spectrum. She equips schools, organizations, and families with practical tools for autism advocacy, resilience, inclusive leadership, and family-centered support. She holds Master Trainer, Master Facilitator, and Instructional Design certifications from the Association for Talent Development (ATD), and is a certified Autism Fundamentals Specialist, a certificate she earned through the University of Nevada, Las Vegas.
